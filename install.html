Install Track Logger

The run-logging app for the Argentina filming trip. Two minutes now, then it works on the mountain with zero signal.

You're not in Safari. An iPhone can only install this from Safari — Chrome and other browsers can't do it.
1

Open the app in Safari

Tap the green button. A dark page called Track Logger opens.

▶  Open Track Logger
2

Tap the Share button

The ⬆︎ square with the arrow, in the bar at the bottom of Safari.

3

Add to Home Screen

Scroll down the share menu, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add (top right). A Track Logger icon appears on your home screen.

4

Open it once from the icon — now, on wifi

That first open stores the whole app on the phone. From then on it works fully offline — no bars needed on the mountain.

During the trip: never delete the icon. The logged runs live inside it — deleting the icon erases them. Back up from the History tab → EXPORT CSV at the end of every day.

On an Android phone instead: open this page in Chrome → menu (⋮) → Install app / Add to Home screen. Everything else is the same.
